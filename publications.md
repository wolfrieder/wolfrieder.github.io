Here is a list of my current publications. You can also find them on my CV (partly) and

and web measurements, with related work spanning security, privacy, and business processes. You may check the [ICORE ranking](https://portal.core.edu.au/conf-ranks/) or [Google Scholar Metrics](https://scholar.google.com/citations?view_op=top_venues&hl=en&vq=en) 
for an external reference on venue reputation and selectivity.
